C. CLASS ARRANGEMENT

  • Each class is 25 minutes long.
  • Classes are conducted as private 1-on-1 sessions.
  • The class schedule will be arranged by mutual agreement between the teacher and parent/student.
  • There is no fixed completion period for the purchased classes unless otherwise agreed upon.
  • The student is expected to attend the agreed class schedule on time.

D. LESSON MATERIALS

  • Learning materials will be prepared by the Teacher based on the student's learning needs, level, and/or the parent's requested topic.
  • Parents may communicate their preferred learning goals or lesson topics before the class.
  • Materials may include PPT lessons, worksheets, reading activities, vocabulary exercises, phonics, grammar, speaking practice, stories, and other appropriate activities.

E. PACKAGE & PAYMENT POLICY

  • The purchased package is intended for one student only and cannot be transferred to another student without the teacher's approval.
  • Once payment has been confirmed and the package has been activated, the classes are considered purchased.
  • Parents are encouraged to communicate any concerns regarding the package or lessons directly with the teacher.

F. STUDENT LEARNING EXPECTATIONS

The student is encouraged to:

  • Attend classes regularly.
  • Participate actively during lessons.
  • Complete assigned activities or practice when applicable.
  • Ask questions whenever they do not understand the lesson.
  • Practice English outside class when possible.

G. CLASS CANCELLATION & RESCHEDULING

  • If the student needs to cancel or reschedule a class, the parent/student should inform the teacher before the scheduled class whenever possible.
  • Rescheduling is subject to the teacher's available schedule.
  • If the student does not attend the class without prior notice, the class may be considered used.
  • If the teacher needs to cancel a class due to an emergency or technical problem, the class will be rescheduled and will not be deducted from the student's package.
  • Once the parent/student and teacher have agreed on a regular class schedule, the selected day and time will remain the student's regular schedule for the following weeks until all purchased class credits have been completed, unless a schedule change is requested and agreed upon by both parties.

H. ASSESSMENT, PROGRESS REPORT & CERTIFICATE

  • Every 10th lesson will serve as an assessment, test, or review to evaluate the student's understanding and progress.
  • After the student completes every 10 lessons, the teacher will provide an Evaluation and Feedback Report summarizing the student's progress, strengths, areas for improvement, and overall performance.
  • Upon successful completion of the enrolled lesson package or program, the student will receive a Certificate of Completion from the teacher.
